# FRANK MANGA+ v1.0.0

The 1.0 release. What started as a decompiled-XAPK experiment in May is now a
complete daily-driver desktop reader for MANGA Plus: full catalog browsing and
search, a distraction-free chapter reader with seamless auto-advance, per-title
layout preferences, resilient image loading — and, as of this release, full
awareness of the MANGA Plus MAX subscription model, from lock badges in the
chapter list to a proactive warning when your plan lapses server-side.

## Subscription awareness (new in this release)

### Real API error messages

- The API's `ErrorResult.english_popup` (subject + body) is now decoded and
  surfaced. A refused chapter says `API error: Invalid user: Invalid user
  access(11301) (action=0)` instead of the old
  `code=Some("action=0") action=None english=None`.
- Fallback order: English popup → `debug_info` → bare action code.

### Visible paywall

- Chapter lists now badge subscription-locked chapters: 🔒 **MAX**
  (standard tier), 🔒 **MAX Deluxe**, or 🔒 **Locked** (locked after free
  read), driven by the API's `chapter_type` field.
- Opening a locked chapter your plan doesn't cover shows a friendly
  "subscription-locked" explanation with a Back-to-title button instead of a
  raw error and a useless Retry.
- Auto-advance that prefetches into a locked chapter now says so, instead of
  the misleading "may be rate-limited" retry loop.

### Plan-drop warning (why chapters suddenly stop loading)

- Root cause documented: the server-side entitlement is backed by a Google
  Play receipt only the official app can refresh; when the attested billing
  period ends the account silently downgrades to `basic` and MAX-tier
  chapters refuse with error 11301.
- The Library page now polls `GET /api/subscription` (at most every 12h) and
  shows a dismissible amber banner when the plan drops below the best plan
  seen, or when the billing period ends without renewing server-side.
- New `get_subscription` Tauri command + `SubscriptionView`/`UserSubscription`
  proto messages (SuccessResult field 36).
- The restore ritual (rooted AVD + official app), including the emulator DNS
  flag and the stale-mitmproxy-setting gotcha, is documented in
  `reader/docs/android-secret.md`.

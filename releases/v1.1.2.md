# FRANK MANGA+ v1.1.2

Chapter-list clarity and a redesigned content-language picker.

## Chapter list no longer looks broken when you're caught up

Reading the newest chapters made the title page look wrong: with "Unread
only" on (the default), every chapter you'd read was hidden, so the list
appeared to *end* at your newest unread chapter (e.g. #122) while "Continue"
jumped to a later, already-read chapter (#131). Nothing was actually wrong —
but there was no way to tell.

- A clear cue now sits above the list whenever read chapters are hidden:
  **"N read chapters hidden · show all"**, one click to reveal them. The
  newest chapters never silently vanish again.
- The count header was reworded. "122 unread of 134" read like a chapter
  position ("chapter 122 of 134"); it's now **"134 total · 12 read · 122
  unread"**, which can't be misread.

Note on totals: 134 is correct — a title's chapter list legitimately
includes bonus ("ex") chapters alongside the numbered ones, and those are
real, readable, and counted.

## Redesigned content-language picker

The row of nine pill buttons in the header (EN ESP FRA IND PT-BR RUS THA VIE
DEU) was replaced with a single compact dropdown: a globe + current-selection
summary ("EN", "EN, ESP", "EN +3", or "All languages") that opens a clean
checklist. It stays a multi-select — pick as many catalogs to merge as you
like — and the last active language can't be switched off, same as before.

## Tests + verification

- New unit coverage for the language-summary label and the reworded
  chapter-count label (159 vitest tests total).
- Dropdown verified in a live dev-server render: panel layout, multi-select
